🌱 Smart Agriculture Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

 📊 Project Overview

**Smart Agriculture Analytics** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze agricultural data and provide data-driven insights for smarter farming decisions.

The project focuses on understanding **crop productivity, environmental conditions, farming practices, and crop health risks**. The dashboard transforms agricultural data into meaningful visual insights that can help identify yield patterns, compare farming practices, evaluate environmental factors, and monitor potential crop-health risks.

---

 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze crop productivity and yield performance.
* Compare crop performance across different regions.
* Study the relationship between crop yield and environmental conditions.
* Analyze irrigation and fertilizer practices.
* Examine pesticide usage and its relationship with crop yield.
* Monitor crop health using NDVI and disease-rate indicators.
* Identify regional and crop-wise patterns to support better farming decisions.

---

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculations and KPI measures
* **Data Visualization**
* **Agricultural Data Analysis**

 📌 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify several important agricultural patterns:

* Crop yield varies across different **regions and crop types**.
* Environmental factors such as **rainfall, soil moisture, temperature, and soil pH** can be compared with crop productivity.
* Different **irrigation and fertilizer practices** show variations in agricultural performance.
* Pesticide usage can be analyzed alongside crop yield to understand farming efficiency.
* **NDVI and disease rate** provide useful indicators for monitoring crop health.
* Interactive filters make it easier to perform **crop-wise and region-wise analysis**.
